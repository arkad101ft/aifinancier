## Title (51 chars)
Why Singapore Is So Rich (It Was Never About Trade)

## Description
Every day, about $1.485 trillion in currency changes hands in Singapore, on an island you can drive across in under an hour. Most people credit the port. The real story is trust.

Liam the Lion takes you from 1965, when Singapore was pushed out of Malaysia with no oil, no farmland and no minerals, to a banker's idea about a gap in the world's trading clock, a deliberately "boring" currency, the Barings collapse, the 1997–98 Asian crisis, today's numbers, the S$3 billion money-laundering case, and what cities like Kigali can learn from it all.
