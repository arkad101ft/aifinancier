# Finance Den Ep. 1 — art direction

Source of truth: the owner's brief (Claude Doc "Finance Den Ep. 1", tab "AI video brief").
This file records how that brief is realised in code; it does not override it.

## Look
- Flat 2D illustration on warm paper (#F7F4EE) with a faint paper-grain overlay.
  Bold dark outlines (navy #13233A, 4-6 px) on characters and key objects. No photos, no gradients
  beyond soft sky washes.
- Palette: paper #F7F4EE, navy #13233A, gold #F5A623, muted blue #5B8DEF.
  Red #D93A3F appears ONLY in Barings (s05) and the laundering case (s08).
- Type: Inter Black for headline numbers and labels, Inter Bold for captions. Numbers are
  written exactly as the narration says them.

## Signature device
"The ledger of trust": a thin gold rule that draws itself under each section title card and
returns once, as the closing checklist in s09. Used sparingly (section swooshes + s09), never as
scaffolding inside scenes.

## Motion
- Slow camera push-in (1.00 -> 1.06) on every shot; a new shot every 3-6 s, cut on narration cues.
- Numbers count up with an ease-out; maps draw their outlines in; stamps slam with overshoot.
- Gold swoosh wipe (0.7 s) between the 10 sections.

## Liam
- Rebuilt as an SVG puppet from the owner's reference: round tan face, orange scalloped mane,
  dot eyes, grey quilted vest over light-blue shirt, gold "FD" badge, tufted tail.
- Expressions: happy, smug (arms crossed), curious (leaning in), wince, impressed, yawn, plus
  pointing and waving arms. Mouth flaps on narration word timings while he is on screen.
- About 10 appearances, in a corner (or centre for the intro and outro).

## Rules carried from the brief
- No real faces: historical people are flat silhouettes with a name caption.
- No real logos or mastheads; headline cards are invented.
- Every on-screen number matches the narration.
- Bottom-right corner left clear for the last 20 s (end-screen elements).
- "FINANCE DEN" tag bottom-left throughout.
