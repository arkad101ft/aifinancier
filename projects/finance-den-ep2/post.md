## Description
On 15 September 2008, Lehman Brothers, a 158-year-old bank, filed the biggest bankruptcy in US history: $619 billion. Within a year, millions had lost their jobs, savings and homes. And it all started with something ordinary: a house.

Liam the Lion opens the biggest file in Crash Files: cheap money and subprime loans, how Wall Street turned mortgages into "AAA" bonds and CDOs, Lehman's 31-to-1 leverage, Bear Stearns at $2 a share, the September that broke the system (Fannie and Freddie, Lehman, AIG, TARP), the damage, and three lessons that still matter.
